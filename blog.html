Quick tips

Quick reads for busy days.

Five small habits our doctors recommend most often.

Walk 30 minutes, most days

Brisk walking supports your heart, your sleep and your mood.

Check your pressure at home

Sit and rest for five minutes, then take two readings and note them down.

Fasting before blood tests

Usually 8 to 10 hours, and water is fine. Ask us if you take regular medicines.

Drink enough water

Two to three litres a day suits most adults, unless your doctor has advised a limit.

Keep vaccines on schedule

Ask your paediatrician for your child's vaccination chart.
