Need help?

Book a visit, or get help right now?

Book an appointment for

  • Routine check-ups and follow-ups
  • Mild fever, cough or stomach upset
  • Reviewing test results
  • Child vaccinations or a child's fever
  • Back, neck or joint pain

Call 108 or go to the nearest emergency room for

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Trouble breathing
  • Sudden weakness or slurred speech
  • Heavy bleeding that will not stop
  • Fainting or severe injury

FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Quick answers before you visit. If yours is not here, ask us and a nurse will reply.

Ask us directly
Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead is the quickest way to see a doctor at your chosen time. We confirm bookings within 30 minutes during opening hours. For tests, mention them when you book so we can guide you on timing.

Do I need to fast before a blood test?

Some tests need a fast and many do not. When you book a test we tell you which applies, so you can plan your morning without guessing.

How soon will I get my reports?

Most common tests are reported the same day. The results board above shows the usual time for each test.

Can I bring my child for a fever?

Yes. Our paediatrics team sees children for fever, vaccinations and routine check-ups. A baby under 3 months with a fever needs urgent care, so call us or go to the nearest emergency room straight away.

Can I use my health insurance?

Yes. Bring your insurance card and we will help you with a cashless claim where your insurer allows it.
